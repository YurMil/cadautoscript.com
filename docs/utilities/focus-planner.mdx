## Premium Productivity, Local Privacy
Focus Planner isn't just another task list; it's a comprehensive workspace built on the "Local-First" philosophy. Your data never leaves your browser, ensuring total privacy while providing powerful tools normally found in cloud-based suites.

### Key Capabilities
*   **Unified Dashboard**: A clean "Welcome Back" interface showing your daily progress, earned rewards, and planned tasks at a glance.
*   **Gamified Success**: Stay motivated with an integrated rewards system. Earn badges as you hit your focus goals and complete planned work.
*   **Calendar Integration**: Plan your time with precision using multiple views (Day, Week, Month, Quarter). Schedule tasks directly onto your calendar.
*   **Integrated Timers**: Start tracking time immediately from your task list. Linked timers ensure every minute of focus is attributed to the right project.
*   **Offline Mastery**: Designed to work entirely offline. Your progress is saved locally and remains available even after browser restarts or page refreshes.

## Workflow Overview
### 1. Plan
Define your categories and tasks in the **Tasks** workspace. Use the **Calendar** to block out time for deep work.
### 2. Focus
Launch the **Dashboard** to see your top priorities for the day. Use the integrated "Play" buttons on task cards to start focus sessions.
### 3. Review
Analyze your performance in the **Analytics** tab. Track focus distribution across categories and watch your reward collection grow.
### 4. Manage
Use **Settings** to customize the appearance, manage backups, or export your data for external use.

## When to Use Focus Planner
*   **Deep Work Sessions:** When you need a distraction-free environment to track high-concentration tasks.
*   **Privacy-First Planning:** When you want a professional tool without the need for an account or cloud sync.
*   **Consolidated Workflow:** When you want your tasks, calendar, and timers in a single, local interface.

## Accuracy and Privacy
*   **Zero Cloud Sync:** No data is sent to external servers. All information stays in your browser's IndexedDB.
*   **Timer Recovery:** The app is engineered to resume active timers automatically after page reloads.
*   **Data Portability:** You are in control of your data. Export your entire workspace as JSON or specific reports as CSV at any time.

## FAQ
*   **Is my data safe?** Yes, it is stored locally in your browser. However, clearing your browser's site data will delete your tasks unless you have exported a backup.
*   **Do I need an account?** No. Just open the utility and start planning.
*   **Can I sync between devices?** Not directly. Use the export/import JSON feature in Settings to move your workspace between machines.
